01 / The story

We learned
the hard way.

Four years ago, two people flew to LA knowing nothing about cars. They came home with a Supra.

Azumi began with stubborn optimism and a third-generation Toyota Supra that experienced mechanics warned us not to buy.

We learned through busted knuckles, bad advice, long nights and every problem the A70 could throw at us. Now the mission is simple: make parts, knowledge and honest build stories easier to find.
